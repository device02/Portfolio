FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a website cost? +

The cost depends on the type of website, number of pages, features and level of customization required. Contact us with your requirements and we'll provide a tailored quote.

How long does it take to build a website? +

A standard business website can typically be completed within 1 to 3 weeks. More complex websites may require additional development and testing time.

Will my website work on phones and tablets? +

Yes. We build responsive websites that adapt to phones, tablets, laptops and desktop computers.

Can you redesign my existing website? +

Yes. We can redesign an existing website to improve its appearance, responsiveness, performance, user experience and overall functionality.

Do you provide website hosting? +

Yes. We can help you select a suitable hosting provider, connect your domain, configure your website and get it properly deployed.

Can I update my website after it is completed? +

Yes. Depending on the type of website we build, we can provide an administration system or ongoing maintenance service that allows your content to be updated.

Do you maintain websites after launch? +

Yes. We offer ongoing website maintenance, updates, troubleshooting, content changes and technical support.

How do I get started? +

Simply contact us and tell us about your business, your goals and the type of website you need. We'll discuss your requirements and recommend the best solution.
